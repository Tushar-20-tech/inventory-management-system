📘 How to Use Inventory Software

⬅ Back to Dashboard

Step 1 — Add Products

Go to the Products section and add product name, category, quantity, and price. This creates your inventory database.

Step 2 — Add Stock (Stock In)

Use Stock In to increase inventory quantity when new products arrive from suppliers.

Step 3 — Remove Stock (Stock Out)

Use Stock Out when products are sold, transferred, or damaged. System automatically updates inventory.

Step 4 — View Reports

Open Reports section to see analytics charts like stock movement, category distribution, and inventory health.

Step 5 — Manage Profile

Click your profile at top-right to view or manage account details securely.
